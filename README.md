# Ibom Christmas Unplugged 4.0 — Logo Design Entry

This repository contains the official logo artwork, vector source files, and exports for the **Ibom Christmas Unplugged 4.0 Logo Design Competition**, organized by the Akwa Ibom State Ministry of Culture and Tourism.

## 🎯 Theme & Design Concept
* **Theme:** Taste of Akwa Ibom: Our Food, Culture and Heritage
* **Concept:** A central map of Akwa Ibom State anchored by key cultural and economic landmarks—Ikot Ekpene raffia craftsmanship, the Uyo Unity Park monument, the historic Oron Ekpu mask, and the coastal beauty of Ibeno/Eket. Framed with festive Christmas ornaments, Ibom Air flights, and the iconic "Land of Promise" banner.
